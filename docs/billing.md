# Billing

## Plans
There are three plans: Free, Pro ($12 per user per month) and Team ($20 per user per month). Annual billing saves two months.

## Refunds
Refunds for annual plans are prorated if you cancel within the first 60 days. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you keep access until the end of the billing period.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first of each month and are also available under Settings → Billing → Invoices as PDF downloads.
