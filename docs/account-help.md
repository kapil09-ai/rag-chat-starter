# Account help

## Reset your password
Go to Settings → Security and choose "Reset password". We email a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or ask support to resend it.

## Turn on two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a one-time code to every sign-in. Open Settings → Security → Two-factor and scan the QR code with an authenticator app. Save the backup codes somewhere safe; each one works once.

## Close your account
You can close your account from Settings → Account → Close account. Your data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, then permanently deleted.
